Privacy

Nothing to see here.

Short version: FileSmith works on your files on your own PC, and neither the app nor this site collects anything about you.

The app

  • Your files never leave your PC. Every conversion, edit, Remove BG and OCR runs locally. FileSmith has no servers to send them to.
  • No analytics, crash reporting, telemetry or account.
  • Update checks: once a day (and when you choose Check for updates…), FileSmith downloads a small public file from this site listing the newest version. The request carries only FileSmith's version and Windows' version, so the server can answer; no ID, no cookie, nothing about your files. Turn it off in Settings → Check for updates.
  • What it stores, on your PC only: your settings (in the Windows registry under HKCU\Software\FileSmith) and a log file at %APPDATA%\FileSmith\filesmith.log that helps with troubleshooting. The log stays on your PC unless you choose to send it to us.

This website

  • No cookies, no analytics, no trackers, no third-party scripts or fonts.
  • The light/dark choice and the wheel colour you pick are saved in your own browser's storage, nowhere else.
  • If you drop a file on the wheel demo, only its name is read, in your browser, to choose the wheel. The file isn't opened or uploaded.
  • The site is hosted on GitHub Pages and the installer on GitHub Releases. Like any web host, GitHub may keep standard server logs such as IP addresses. See GitHub's privacy statement.
