Changelog

What's new

Every release, newest first. Installing a newer version over an older one keeps your settings, your startup entry and your right-click entry.

1.9.0

Latest26 October 2026
  • FileSmith updates itself. It checks for new versions once a day, shows what's new, and installs with one click, or automatically if you turn that on in Settings.
  • Updates never interrupt your work: FileSmith waits for conversions and exports to finish and asks before closing any open window.
  • Every update is verified before it runs, with a signature only FileSmith's publisher can make.

1.8.5

25 October 2026

New

  • Timeline video editor. Every clip on one timeline, playback with sound, voice and music tracks with waveforms, split, trim and drag, speed, crop, fades, and export as a video, a GIF or a frame.
  • Pro PDF tools. Organize pages, Merge, page numbers, watermark, and adding or removing a password.
  • Editor and tool windows are proper app windows: a taskbar button, Alt+Tab, minimize from the taskbar, and the file name in the title.

Fixed since 1.8.0

  • The editor's picture could freeze on screen, with buttons not where they looked.
  • Space and the arrow keys stopped working after clicking the timeline.
  • Playback could get stuck, or play sound without a picture.

1.8.1 – 1.8.4

25 October 2026
  • The editor stays smooth on long, heavy videos (an hour of 1080p60, or 1920×1200 60 fps recordings): sound on its own thread, thumbnails on demand, cached waveforms, and hardware decoding for the preview.
  • Playback can't get stuck. If the sound device disappears or changes, playback carries on silently instead of stopping, and anything that fails is shown and logged.
  • The editor opens as a solid window that fades in without moving, so what you see always matches where the buttons are.
  • If FileSmith ever freezes, it writes a report you can send in.

1.8.0

25 October 2026
  • The timeline editor: clips side by side with thumbnails, each clip's sound as a waveform, extra voice and music tracks, an inspector, undo and redo for everything, and export.
  • Playback with sound across every clip, with picture and sound in sync.
  • One Edit video segment on the wheel replaces separate Trim, Make GIF and Edit video entries.

1.7.0

25 October 2026
  • Pro PDF tool windows: Organize pages, Merge, page numbers, watermark, and add or remove a password, each with a live preview of the real page.
  • FileSmith's own PDF → Word engine: fonts and styles, columns, headings, lists, headers and footers with page numbers, ruled tables, links, text wrapping around pictures, and pictures at their original quality.
  • DOCX + OCR: scanned pages become editable text, fully offline. It handles busy scans like ID cards and hall tickets.
  • The video editor window: trim, GIF, frame, crop and rotate, speed, sound, and join clips.
  • The DOCX group on the Convert page sits at the top of the wheel, so its fan opens upward.

1.5.0

22 October 2026
  • FileWheel is now FileSmith. Installing over FileWheel upgrades it in place and keeps your settings.
  • Split window: each page, every N pages, equal parts, your own cuts, picked pages (into several PDFs), or by chapter.
  • Slides → PDF and images, using PowerPoint if it's installed, otherwise LibreOffice.
  • Smart Remove BG: ink is lifted cleanly off paper, and photos go to the offline AI model.

1.4.0

21 October 2026
  • Tool groups that fan out: drag over a group and its tools pop out as pills around the wheel.
  • Remove BG, running offline on your PC.
  • Zip and extract: zip files and whole folders, and extract archives safely.
  • The glass looks open faster.

1.3.3

21 October 2026
  • The first public build: drag and tap Shift to open the wheel; right-click → Open with FileSmith; conversions for images, PDFs, documents, video and audio; crop, resize, Add BG and more; 12 colour themes, 5 glass looks and custom colours.
